AI Social Manager

AI Social Manager is a locally operated marketing workspace by BELINA Apps for preparing campaigns, organizing media and reviewing posts.

Your campaigns. Your content. Your approval.

AI Social Manager is a locally operated application by BELINA Apps. It helps you organize your own photographs and videos, prepare marketing campaigns and individual posts, plan dates in a calendar, and review or approve drafts before any external action.

What you can prepare

Keep media in a persistent local library, assign it to campaigns, choose its order and schedule, and prepare platform-specific captions and hashtags. Review posts individually or approve a campaign. Optional AI assistance and Creative Studio image generation require configuration and visible confirmation before paid requests.

Your workspace runs on a computer or server you control. This public website introduces the product and provides support and legal information; it is not the application dashboard and does not upload your local media library.

Contact BELINA Apps

TikTok integration: in development

The TikTok integration is currently in development and is not offered here as an approved, publicly available publishing service. Connecting an account requires a configured TikTok Developer App, the relevant permissions granted by TikTok and explicit authorization from the account owner through official TikTok Login Kit.

Planned account connection and content transfers depend on TikTok review, approved scopes and platform rules. Approving a draft alone does not authorize sending it to TikTok: a separate explicit transfer authorization is required. These pages do not claim that an account is connected, that TikTok has approved the integration or that a post has been published.

Instagram and Facebook are currently campaign-planning targets only; publishing integrations are not available.

AI and paid operations

Optional OpenAI analysis and image generation require a separate visible confirmation before each paid request. The workspace uses server-side credentials. Missing media are proposed for creation; they are not generated automatically.

If a request has an uncertain result, check its history and the provider's billing before trying again. A local budget reservation is not a provider-enforced maximum charge.

Local workspace and your control

Media files and campaign data are retained on your local server. Closing your browser does not delete saved work. Optional external AI requests send only the inputs described for the operation after your confirmation. See the Privacy Policy for storage, provider transfers, retention and deletion controls.

For help with local data or integration setup, contact belina.apps@gmail.com.
