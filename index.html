Help with AI Social Manager, a user-controlled marketing workspace by BELINA Apps.

Your content. Your approval.

Prepare campaigns using your own photos and videos, review AI-assisted copy, and decide what can be sent to TikTok. Approving a draft and authorizing a TikTok transfer are separate steps.

Contact BELINA Apps

For support, privacy requests or questions about these terms, email belina.apps@gmail.com. English and Czech messages are welcome.

Please include your app version, the operation that failed and the displayed error code. Do not send API keys, access or refresh tokens, passwords, your environment file, or private media unless it is necessary and you choose to share it.

TikTok connection and posting

Connecting an account requires a configured TikTok Developer App, the relevant approved scopes and the account owner's authorization through official TikTok Login Kit. Public Direct Post additionally depends on TikTok review and its rules for the intended use of the API client. This website does not confirm approval or endorsement by TikTok.

A successful transfer is not proof of publication. An inbox draft must be completed in TikTok. The application displays publication only when TikTok reports a completed post. Video inbox transfers cannot carry a caption through the available endpoint; the workspace provides a copy button for the prepared text.

Instagram and Facebook currently support campaign planning only. They are not connected for publishing in the current local version.

AI and paid operations

Optional OpenAI analysis and image generation require a separate visible confirmation before each paid request. The workspace uses server-side credentials. Missing media are proposed for creation; they are not generated automatically.

If a request has an uncertain result, check its history and the provider's billing before trying again. A local budget reservation is not a provider-enforced maximum charge.

Local data and account disconnection

The current workspace runs on a local server you control. Closing a browser does not delete saved campaigns or files. A separately running worker can process transfers you previously authorized. Disconnecting TikTok stops waiting jobs and requests revocation; a failed revocation is shown explicitly. It does not delete posts already on TikTok.

For help removing local data, contact belina.apps@gmail.com. Published social content and data retained by outside providers must be managed with those providers as well.
