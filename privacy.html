Privacy Policy

How AI Social Manager handles local workspace data, optional OpenAI requests and authorized TikTok operations.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who is responsible and what this covers

AI Social Manager is software by Vít Bělina, operating as BELINA Apps, Czechia. For privacy matters, contact belina.apps@gmail.com. This policy covers the current local-server version and its public support website. It does not replace the separate StickerVerse or CLIPIT privacy policies.

The current version does not automatically send your workspace to a central BELINA Apps application backend. Your installation runs on a computer or server you control. You or your organization decide which brand information, media and accounts to use. BELINA Apps is responsible for personal data it receives through support correspondence. An organization operating its own installation is responsible for its own processing of uploaded content and any additional hosting or logging it introduces.

2. Data handled by the workspace

  • Brand and campaign information: brand name, products, goals, communication style, audience descriptions, prompts, captions, hashtags, schedules and approval records.
  • Media: photos, videos, logos, screenshots and other files you select, their names, formats, dimensions, checksums, thumbnails, generated outputs and export copies. Images and videos may themselves contain personal data.
  • TikTok information, when authorized: account identifier, display name and avatar URL; granted scopes; access and refresh tokens and their expirations; available posting settings; publication identifiers, processing results and errors. Basic account statistics are requested only with the corresponding permission.
  • Operational records: confirmation and session metadata, campaign changes, transfer jobs, attempts, provider request identifiers where available, AI responses and local cost/reservation records.

We do not ask you to enter your TikTok password in AI Social Manager. Official TikTok Login Kit handles login and authorization.

3. Purposes and legal basis

The workspace uses the information you provide to prepare content, organize media and schedules, apply your approvals, carry out specifically authorized provider requests, account connections and transfers, show actual provider status, and record costs and errors. Providing the relevant data is necessary for the feature you choose; you can leave optional integrations unused.

Where BELINA Apps processes support correspondence to provide a requested service, the legal basis is performance of a contract or steps requested before a contract, where applicable (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Necessary support administration, security and handling legal claims may rely on legitimate interests in providing a safe, functioning service and resolving requests (Article 6(1)(f)). Applicable legal obligations may require processing under Article 6(1)(c). Where consent is the applicable basis, it is optional and can be withdrawn for future processing (Article 6(1)(a)).

In-app confirmation of an API operation is a technical permission for that operation. It does not automatically establish a lawful basis to upload someone else's personal data. The operator must have an appropriate basis and rights for the material they choose to process.

4. Local storage and security

Selected originals and persistent results are stored on the local server's disk. Media metadata, campaigns, brand profiles, AI runs, approvals, TikTok account records and publishing history are stored in local SQLite. AI budget records are stored locally as well. The database and media are not automatically end-to-end encrypted by the application.

TikTok access and refresh tokens are encrypted on the server with authenticated encryption; the encryption key and provider API keys stay in server-side configuration and are not sent to the browser. Protect the device, server configuration and backups. These controls do not guarantee absolute security.

5. Optional OpenAI processing

Only after you confirm a paid AI operation does the server send its stated input to OpenAI. Campaign preparation can include your saved brand profile, campaign goal, selected filenames and resized copies of selected photographs. Videos are not sent for frame or audio analysis in the current campaign workflow.

Image generation sends the prompt and the reference images you explicitly select. An AI edit can also send the prior generated background. Original logos and screenshots selected only as exact local composition layers are processed locally instead of being sent as AI references. Generated outputs and usage information return to the local server.

OpenAI operates its own service. Its processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements are governed by its applicable terms, account settings and privacy information. Do not assume that deleting a local copy deletes provider-held data.

6. TikTok authorization, posting and public media links

When you connect TikTok, the server exchanges the authorization code for tokens with TikTok and can refresh them to keep your authorized connection working. It requests only the scopes configured for the Developer App; TikTok may grant fewer. Account information comes from TikTok, and statistics are shown only when supported and returned under the granted permissions.

A draft approval does not itself send media to TikTok. A separate confirmation authorizes a specific transfer, including the account, content, settings and timing. The server worker can then run without an open browser. For a concept sent to your inbox, you complete the post in TikTok; Direct Post additionally requires the necessary permission and platform conditions.

For TikTok's pull-from-URL transfer, specifically authorized export copies are made available on the configured verified HTTPS media endpoint at random, hard-to-guess URLs. These links are publicly retrievable by anyone who has them, not password-protected. Link access expires after 48 hours. The worker removes expired export files when its cleanup runs. Originals are not replaced.

TikTok receives the selected export files and supported posting metadata, and returns transfer/processing status and publication identifiers when available. It handles that data under its own Privacy Policy and applicable platform terms. Disconnecting the local app does not delete content already sent to TikTok.

7. Retention and deletion controls

  • Persistent local workspace: originals, saved results, campaigns, profiles, AI responses, job history and local cost records remain until the installation operator removes them. Expiration of a confirmation prevents its use; it does not necessarily remove the historical database row.
  • Temporary Creative Studio work: unsaved working assets expire after at most two hours of availability and are cleared by server restart. Saving a result into the persistent library creates a separate retained copy.
  • Session cookies: the local workspace uses an HttpOnly session cookie with a two-hour maximum age. The TikTok OAuth binding cookie has a ten-minute maximum age. The browser may remove cookies earlier.
  • TikTok tokens: encrypted tokens remain while the account is connected. Successful disconnection removes the stored tokens and requests revocation. If revocation fails, the interface says so and retains the encrypted connection for retry. A separate recovery action can remove the local connection after you confirm manual permission removal in TikTok; it does not claim API-verified revocation.
  • Media exports: link access lasts up to 48 hours; expired file cleanup requires the worker to run. Other server copies, device backups and copies held by TikTok are separate.
  • Support email: correspondence is retained as needed to answer and resolve the request, and where necessary to meet legal obligations or establish or defend legal claims. You can request deletion when retention is no longer necessary.

You can stop future optional AI requests by not confirming them, cancel waiting transfer jobs, disconnect TikTok, and stop the worker. These actions do not guarantee cancellation of a request already accepted by a provider. Contact us for help with removing local workspace data; there is no claim that a single account-disconnect button erases all records or backups.

8. Sharing, providers and international processing

The current software does not sell your workspace data or use it for advertising. It does not add an advertising or third-party analytics SDK to the workspace. Data can be disclosed to OpenAI and TikTok for the operations described above, to the hosting and email providers needed for the website or support, or when legally required. Your own cloud backup, proxy or hosting choices may add other recipients.

External providers may process data outside your country and outside the European Economic Area. Their notices and applicable agreements explain their processing locations and available safeguards. The local software does not guarantee a particular data residency. Review the provider arrangements appropriate to your account before sending personal data; this policy does not itself constitute a data processing or international-transfer agreement.

10. Your rights and choices

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Rights can be subject to lawful conditions and exceptions. Contact belina.apps@gmail.com to make a request; we may need proportionate information to verify identity and locate records we actually hold.

You can also contact your supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, or an appropriate authority in the EEA. For data held solely by your own installation or by a separate provider, that operator or provider may need to handle the request.

The workspace proposes marketing content; it does not make solely automated decisions intended to have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. Outputs, terms and transfers remain subject to human review.
